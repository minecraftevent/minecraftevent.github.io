AquaLAN Minecraft Building Competition

How to Switch Minecraft Versions

In order to access the AquaLAN Minecraft server, you'll need to use version 26.2 This video below will walk you through the process of switching versions.

Commands

/plot autoClaims a random plot to build on.
/plot claimClaims a plot of ur choice to build on.
/plot middleTeleport back to the centre of your plot.
/plot clearWipe your plot and start fresh.
/spawnReturn to the spawn area.

Rules

  • You don't need to be signed up to build, but only signed-up contestants are eligible to win — sign up anytime at the front desk.
  • Claiming a plot without signing up may result in losing your plot or a ban if it causes problems, e.g. taking a spot from a signed-up contestant.
  • Keep it respectful — no swearing, hate speech, racism, or bullying, in chat or in-game.
  • No offensive or explicit builds — this includes symbols, imagery, and anything not appropriate for an all-ages event.
  • Build only inside your own plot. Griefing another player's plot will result in immediate disqualification and a ban from the server.
  • Griefing the server itself will result in immediate disqualification and a ban from the server.
  • No mods that assist building by copying or overlaying pre-made designs (e.g. Schematica, Litematica) — reference images for inspiration are fine.
  • Ask a staff member if anything's unclear or feels wrong.

Judging

After judging begins, a staff panel reviews every finished build and decides the final placements. All plots will be locked from further building once judging starts at 9:30 PM on 29th of September, so nobody can make late changes after being judged.
